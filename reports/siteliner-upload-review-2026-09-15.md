## Use the correct measurement column

The CSV contains both `Match Percentage` and `Match Percentage (with common)`.
The consultant's 37-page list uses the former. For example, Wise vs Remitly is
79% in the first column and 94% with common content. Do not compare the second
column with the original first-column baseline, or compare either directly with
the repository's local 10-word shingle metric. A valid before/after comparison
needs consistent settings and a comparable crawl corpus.

## What the noindex rows mean for this audit

All 72 SiteLiner noindex URLs still exist and emit meta noindex in the current
local build. None of the 37 rewrite targets is in that skipped set. This is a
separate indexability policy issue, not proof that the 37 target rewrites were
blocked by noindex. Review these URLs against the site's intended indexing
allowlists and demand data; do not bulk-remove the directives solely because
SiteLiner skipped them.

The five canonical skips are not automatically broken pages. Check the intended
canonical destinations rather than treating a skip reason as an error count.
