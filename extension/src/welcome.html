SendMoneyCompare is ready

See what money transfer providers pay out for your amount, from any page.

1

Pin it to your toolbar

Click the puzzle piece at the top right of your browser, then the pin next to SendMoneyCompare. It stays one click away.

2

Check prices automatically

On Wise, Remitly, Western Union and other money transfer providers, a small panel tells you whether the transfer you're setting up is a good deal, and who pays more. It reads only the amount and currencies on the page, never runs on banks, and never edits provider forms or existing links.

3

Or open it anywhere

Click SendMoneyCompare to compare any amount, convert currencies and follow rates.

4

Three shortcuts

  • Alt+Shift+S opens it
  • Select an amount like $1,250 on any page, right-click, and compare or convert it
  • Type smc, a space, then 500 gbp inr in the address bar

Help improve SendMoneyCompare?

Share usage analytics: which tabs you open, which routes you compare and which providers you choose, with a random ID made for this browser. Usage analytics is off unless you say yes, and you can change it any time in Settings.
